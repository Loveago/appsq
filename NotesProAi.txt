# AI Second Brain — Final Product Draft

## 1. Product Vision

Build a beautiful, intelligent note-taking app for **Android and iOS** that acts as the user's personal AI second brain.

The app should allow users to capture thoughts naturally through text, voice, images, and recordings. Instead of simply storing information, the AI understands the content, extracts context, creates tasks and lists, connects related information, and helps users retrieve their knowledge later.

### Core Promise

> **Capture anything. Your AI organizes the rest.**

The experience should feel fast, minimal, modern and premium — not like a traditional notes application.

---

# 2. Subscription Model

## Free Plan — $0/month

The free version should be genuinely useful and not feel like a crippled trial.

### Included

* Unlimited basic text notes
* Basic note organization
* AI note summarization
* Basic context extraction
* AI-generated lists
* Basic task extraction
* AI-powered note search
* Limited AI conversations
* Voice-to-text
* Basic reminders
* Basic projects
* Standard AI models
* Ads

### Free AI Usage

Free users receive a reasonable monthly AI allowance.

When the allowance is reached, they can:

* Wait for the allowance to reset
* Upgrade to Pro

The exact limits should be configurable from the backend rather than hard-coded into the app.

# 4. Advertising Strategy

Ads should **never destroy the note-taking experience**.

The goal is to monetize free users while keeping the product pleasant.

## Where ads can appear

### Home Screen

A small native/sponsored card can occasionally appear between content sections.

Example:

> **Sponsored**
>
> Discover a productivity tool that helps you stay organized.
>
> Learn More →

It should visually match the application's design.

### Notes List

Occasionally insert a small native ad between groups of notes.

### AI Usage / Upgrade Moment

When a free user reaches an AI limit, show the upgrade screen rather than immediately forcing an ad.

Example:

> You've used your free AI allowance.
>
> **Unlock unlimited AI with Pro**
>
> $4.99/month
>
> [Upgrade to Pro]
>
> Continue with Free

### Important Advertising Rules

Never:

* Show an ad immediately when opening the app
* Show an ad while the user is typing
* Interrupt voice recording
* Interrupt a meeting
* Cover notes
* Display pop-up ads repeatedly
* Show ads after every action
* Interrupt an AI response
* Put ads inside private/personal content

Ads should be **native, sparse and contextual**.

# 22. AI Architecture

The backend should use a provider-independent AI layer.

```text
Flutter App
     │
     ▼
NestJS API
     │
     ▼
AI Service Layer
     │
 ┌───┴──────────────┐
 │                  │
AI Provider A    AI Provider B
 │                  │
 └───────┬──────────┘
         ▼
       Models
```

AI providers should be configurable through environment variables.

This allows the application to switch between OpenAI-compatible providers without rewriting the mobile application.

## AI Operations

The backend should provide separate AI services:

* `summarizeNote()`
* `extractContext()`
* `extractTasks()`
* `createList()`
* `answerFromNotes()`
* `generateBriefing()`
* `analyzeMeeting()`
* `rewriteNote()`
* `semanticSearch()`

AI responses should use validated structured output wherever possible.

**Notes + AI + Tasks + Memory + Personal Knowledge**

**Notes + Chatbot**
